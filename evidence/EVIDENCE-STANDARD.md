# Evidence Standard

## Required for every public claim
1. State the claim precisely.
2. Link the source artifact, commit, test report, log, or experiment record.
3. Explain how the evidence was collected.
4. State environment, date, sample size and relevant assumptions.
5. Disclose failures, exclusions, and limitations.
6. Distinguish direct measurements from estimates and qualitative observations.

## Approved labels
- **Measured:** captured by a stated measurement method.
- **Verified:** checked against a criterion or reproduced.
- **Observed:** documented qualitative observation.
- **Estimated:** calculated from stated assumptions.
- **Planned:** not yet executed.
- **Not available:** the tool does not expose the data.

## Do not
- Invent token counts, savings, costs, test results, benchmarks, or time savings.
- Treat test coverage as proof of correctness.
- Claim a tool or hook executed without evidence.
- Claim HIPAA compliance or security certification for this educational app.
- Publish secrets, personal data, real patient data, or confidential employer materials.
- Compare runs with materially different task scopes without explaining the difference.
